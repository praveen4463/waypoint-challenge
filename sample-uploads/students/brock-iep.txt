Narrative Description of School District Proposal
1. What action is the school district proposing to take?
The school district is proposing the supports, services and placement outlined in the enclosed IEP and Placement forms.
2. Why is the school district proposing to act?
The school district met with the parent and school-based Team on 11/17/25 for a regularly scheduled Annual Review
Team Meeting. The Team reviewed Brock's progress and continued areas of need and developed the enclosed IEP for
continued support and services.
3. What rejected options were considered and why was each option rejected?
The team considered a substantially separate placement for Brock; however, this option was rejected because Brock
performs at or above grade level in math, science, and history when reading material is provided in audio or oral format.
The team also considered placing Brock in a co-taught ELA section without pull-out intervention; this was rejected
because direct, structured decoding intervention is required to address Brock's specific learning disability in reading.
4. What evaluation procedure, test, record or report was used as a basis for the proposed action?
Brock's most recent re-evaluation was completed on 10/22/2025 and included the Woodcock-Johnson IV Tests of
Achievement, the Comprehensive Test of Phonological Processing (CTOPP-2), iReady reading and math, classroom-based
work samples, and observation. Reports, grades, and progress toward IEP goals were also used.
5. What other factors were relevant to the school district's decision?
Teacher input, parent input, and student input were all relevant. The Team also considered Chapter 71 of the
Massachusetts General Laws. The Team developed a self-regulation goal based on Brock's pattern of using humor and
disruption as avoidance behaviors when frustrated by reading demands.
6. What next steps, if any, are recommended?
The school district will initiate the supports and services outlined in the enclosed IEP once parental consent is received.
